About

Astor BNB is a family-run hospitality project rooted in Thessaloniki. Our journey in tourism began in 1980 through ASTOR TRAVEL.

We carefully acquire apartments and transform them through thoughtful renovation, creating welcoming spaces filled with dedication and love.

Each home carries a unique touch inspired by experiences from life and invites guests to discover them.

ASTOR BNB proudly operates as a sister company of ASTOR TRAVEL.
