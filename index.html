Our Story

Built on heritage.
Crafted with love.

Since 1980, the Astor family has been devoted to a single pursuit: transforming forgotten spaces into homes worthy of remembrance. We are not developers — we are craftsmen. Each property we acquire is restored with intention and brought to life with a character entirely its own.

We believe a great stay begins long before a guest arrives — in the choice of materials, in the light through a restored window, in the quiet detail that makes a space feel deeply human. Every home in our collection carries a story, and we consider it our duty to tell it well.

People remember how a place made them feel. That understanding guides everything we do — from the first brick laid to the final flourish of a furnished room.

Astor BnB is the proud sister company of Astor Travel Agency — together, we offer a complete journey from the moment you book to the moment you arrive home.
