About

Astor BNB is a family-run hospitality project rooted in Thessaloniki. Our journey in tourism began in 1980 through ASTOR TRAVEL. We carefully acquire apartments and renovate them with dedication and love, creating welcoming spaces for visitors. ASTOR BNB proudly operates as a sister company of ASTOR TRAVEL.
