Our Story

Built on heritage.
Crafted with love.

Since 1980, the Astor family has been devoted to a single pursuit: transforming forgotten spaces into homes worthy of remembrance. We are not developers — we are craftsmen. Each property we acquire is approached as a labour of love, carefully restored with intention, and brought to life with a character entirely its own.

We believe that a great stay begins long before a guest arrives. It begins in the choice of materials, in the light that falls through a restored window, in the quiet detail that makes a space feel lived-in and deeply human. Every home in our collection carries a story — and we consider it our duty to tell it well.

Four decades of hospitality have taught us that people remember how a place made them feel. That understanding guides everything we do — from the first brick laid to the final flourish of a furnished room.

Astor BnB is the proud sister company of Astor Travel Agency —
together, we offer a complete journey from the moment you book to the moment you arrive home.
