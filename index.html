About

Astor BNB is a family-run hospitality project rooted in Thessaloniki. Our journey in tourism began in 1980 through our parent company, ASTOR TRAVEL. For decades we have welcomed travelers from around the world, always guided by the belief that authentic hospitality begins with care and attention to detail.

We carefully acquire apartments and transform them through thoughtful renovation. Every home is redesigned with patience, dedication, and a genuine love for creating welcoming spaces. Each property carries its own subtle touch inspired by moments, places, and experiences from life.

When guests stay with us, they are not simply visiting a place to sleep — they are discovering a space shaped by stories, memories, and a passion for hospitality.

ASTOR BNB proudly operates as a sister company of ASTOR TRAVEL.
